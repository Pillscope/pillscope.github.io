About PillScope

A faster way to understand what you're taking

PillScope is a simple medicine lookup tool — search a name and get the essentials: what it's for, how it's taken, and what to watch out for.

What is PillScope?

PillScope is a lightweight reference tool for looking up common medicines. Type a drug name, generic name, or category into the search bar and results appear instantly — no forms, no waiting, no extra clicks.

Each medicine page lays out the same core information in the same order every time: what it treats, typical dosage, common side effects, and important warnings — so you always know where to look.

Why it exists

Reading a prescription label or a pharmacy insert can be dense and hard to skim quickly. PillScope exists to strip that information down to what people usually want to know first — plainly written, and easy to scan on any device.

Who it's for

  • Anyone who wants a quick refresher on a medicine before taking it
  • Caregivers keeping track of medications for someone else
  • Students and curious readers exploring how common drugs work

How search works

Type into the search bar on the Home page, then press enter or tap the magnifying-glass icon to see results. That takes you to a full results page matching against a medicine's brand name, generic name, and category, refined further as you keep typing there — plus live suggestions pulled from RxNorm and openFDA for names not in the local list. Selecting a result takes you straight to its medicine page.

Not medical advice. PillScope is an informational reference only. It doesn't replace guidance from a doctor or pharmacist — always consult a healthcare professional before starting, stopping, or changing any medication.
