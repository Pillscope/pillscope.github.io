Scan a medicine barcode

Point your camera at the barcode on the box or bottle.

Requesting camera access…

This works for the National Drug Code (NDC) that most US pharmacy packaging encodes in its barcode. It won't recognize every possible consumer barcode (e.g. imported products or supplements with no NDC) — for that you'd need a separate paid barcode-lookup service. Camera scanning also requires HTTPS and camera permission, and works best in Chrome/Edge/Android browsers.
